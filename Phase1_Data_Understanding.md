# Phase 1: Data Understanding & Problem Definition

## Business Overview
The business is a global retail superstore that sells various products (Furniture, Office Supplies, Technology) to customers worldwide.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
1. **Total Sales**: Revenue generated from orders.
2. **Total Profit**: Net profit after costs and discounts.
3. **Profit Margin %**: (Profit / Sales) * 100.
4. **Average Delivery Delay**: Difference between Ship Date and Order Date.
5. **Discount Impact**: Relationship between discount levels and profit/sales.
6. **Customer RFM**: Recency, Frequency, and Monetary value of customers.

## Business Problems
1. **Loss-Making Regions**: Identify regions where profit is negative and investigate the cause (high shipping costs, high discounts, etc.).
2. **High Sales, Low Profit Categories**: Find categories that drive revenue but fail to contribute to the bottom line.
3. **Discount Optimization**: Determine the optimal discount threshold that maximizes sales without destroying profit.
4. **Shipping Performance**: Identify shipping modes or regions with frequent delays and their impact on profit.
5. **Customer Segmentation**: Group customers into segments (Champions, At-Risk, etc.) to tailor marketing strategies.
6. **Product Portfolio Analysis**: Identify products that are consistently underperforming across all markets.
7. **Market Trends**: Analyze seasonal sales patterns to optimize inventory.
8. **Top Customers**: Identify high-value customers for loyalty programs.
